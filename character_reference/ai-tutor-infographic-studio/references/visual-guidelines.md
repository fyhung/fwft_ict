# Visual and QA guidelines

## Identity anchors

- Preserve long black hair, braided side detail, dark round glasses, fair complexion, and a youthful adult tutor appearance.
- Prefer `realistic-head-pose.png` for the face and hair construction.
- Use outfit references for clothing only; do not let them override the canonical face.

## Educational composition

- Reserve roughly 65–75% of the page for teaching content.
- Use a clear title, short section headings, diagrams, examples, and visible reading order.
- Avoid walls of text. Prefer concise labels, arrows, tables, and grouped concepts.
- Maintain strong foreground/background contrast and mobile-readable text.

## Language

- Use Traditional Chinese, not Simplified Chinese.
- Pair important terminology as `中文（English）`.
- Verify spelling, capitalization, and curriculum meaning.
- Reject pseudo-text, duplicated labels, invented acronyms, and malformed characters.

## Generation QA

Reject or revise when any identity anchor drifts, required coverage is missing, the aspect ratio is wrong, the character occupies too much space, or important text cannot be read at normal viewing size.
