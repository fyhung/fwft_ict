\#Outfit

The Signature Outfit is the permanent default appearance of the AI Tutor. It is \*not\* a school uniform or a corporate business suit, but a timeless \*Designer Academic Uniform\*. Materials use premium matte woven fabrics, the blouse uses soft matte cotton, the bow is satin silk, and all metallic elements are Champagne Gold. The outfit should always communicate quiet luxury through material quality rather than excessive ornamentation.
