AI Tutor — Signature Outfit v2.0
Design Philosophy

The Signature Outfit represents the permanent visual identity of the AI Tutor. Rather than resembling either a conventional school uniform or a corporate business suit, it is conceived as a timeless Designer Academic Uniform that embodies knowledge, warmth, elegance and hope.

Version 2.0 introduces the Academic Mantle, replacing the cropped jacket as the defining garment of the outfit. Inspired by the ceremonial shoulder forms of Oxford Academic Hoods and Cambridge Academic Regalia, the mantle is reinterpreted into a contemporary Luxury Academia silhouette.

The design language combines Luxury Academia, Soft Fantasy, Modern Minimalism and Quiet Luxury. Every detail exists for symbolic and compositional reasons rather than decoration. The outfit should feel premium, refined and instantly recognizable from both the front and the back.

Colour Language

The outfit continues to follow the established four-colour palette.

Pearl White —50%,  purity, knowledge, calmness
Lavender Mist — 35%, wisdom, gentleness, signature recognition
Sakura Pink — 10%, restrained emotional warmth
Champagne Gold — 5%, refined metallic accent

The palette remains soft, desaturated and luxurious. No highly saturated colours should be introduced into the signature outfit.

Overall Silhouette

Version 2.0 shifts the visual balance from tailoring toward sculptural shoulder architecture.

The oversized signature bow remains the primary focal point after the face and glasses.

The newly introduced Academic Mantle becomes the second defining visual element, creating a broader and more memorable shoulder silhouette while allowing the waistline to remain clean and youthful.

The overall silhouette communicates intelligence, elegance and confidence without appearing formal or authoritarian.

Academic Mantle (NEW Signature Element)

The Academic Mantle replaces the former Cropped Academia Jacket and becomes the defining structural element of the Signature Outfit.

It is not a cape, shawl or poncho.

It is a single-piece sculptural shoulder garment inspired by traditional academic regalia but redesigned through a modern luxury fashion perspective.

Inspiration

Length

The mantle is intentionally very short.

It covers only:

shoulders
collarbones
upper chest
upper back

It must never cover:

waist
oversized signature bow
arms
body movement

The blouse and bow remain fully visible beneath it.

Construction

The mantle is constructed from a single continuous panel.

It is never:

layered
ruffled
cape-like
folded
double-panelled

Instead, it forms one carefully sculpted three-dimensional piece.

The simplicity is essential to its elegance.

Shoulder Proportion

The shoulder line extends approximately 20% wider than a conventional blouse.

The purpose is not exaggerated fashion but stronger character recognition.

This wider shoulder silhouette gives the character greater visual presence while preserving a delicate overall body proportion.

Front Profile

The front edge remains almost horizontal.

Only a gentle downward curve is permitted.

Sharp V-shapes or pointed fronts are prohibited.

The oversized bow must always remain the visual centre of the composition.

The mantle frames the bow rather than competing with it.

Rear Profile

The rear silhouette is the defining signature of Version 2.0.

Beginning from the shoulders, the mantle gradually narrows toward the centre back before terminating in a clean inverted triangular point.

This distinctive rear silhouette serves as one of the strongest visual identifiers of the character.

Even from behind, the character should be immediately recognizable.

Edge Finish

The perimeter is outlined by an extremely thin Lavender Mist piping measuring approximately 2–3 mm.

The piping should remain understated and elegant.

It reinforces the silhouette without becoming decorative.

Embroidery

Embroidery remains intentionally restrained.

Only a subtle Champagne Gold Star Trail is permitted near selected edges.

Large printed graphics or repetitive decorative star patterns are not permitted.

Every embroidered star should retain symbolic meaning.

Lavender Blouse

The blouse remains largely unchanged.

Constructed from premium matte cotton with an extremely pale lavender tone, it serves as a quiet visual foundation beneath both the Academic Mantle and the oversized signature bow.

Its purpose is to support the overall composition rather than dominate it.

Signature Two-Tone Bow

The oversized signature bow remains the primary visual identity after the face and glasses.

Its overall size and proportions remain unchanged.

The satin construction continues to contrast against the matte textures of both the blouse and the Academic Mantle.

The outer layer uses Lavender Mist satin.

The inner folds reveal Sakura Pink.

The central Champagne Gold five-pointed star ornament remains the symbolic heart of the outfit.

The Academic Mantle is intentionally designed so that it frames the bow without obscuring it.

Pleated Skirt

The pleated skirt continues the Luxury Academia aesthetic.

Its high-waisted silhouette balances the broader shoulder architecture introduced by the Academic Mantle.

The Lavender Mist matte woven fabric provides visual harmony while maintaining natural movement.

The Star Trail embroidery continues around the hem using restrained Champagne Gold stitching.

Waist Ornament

The delicate Champagne Gold chain with star charm and tassel remains unchanged.

It provides gentle movement while reinforcing the symbolic language of aspiration and continuous learning.

Signature Accessories

The accessory system remains unchanged.

Twin Star Earrings
Magic Fountain Pen
Mini AI Notebook

Together they reinforce the character's educational identity while complementing the visual language established by the outfit.

Footwear

Preferred footwear remains:

White luxury Mary Jane shoes
Minimalist white luxury loafers

Both continue to emphasize professionalism balanced with warmth.

Very light pearl-white matte tights complete the silhouette.

Material Language

Material selection remains fundamental to the Quiet Luxury philosophy.

Academic Mantle — premium matte woven fabric with sculptural body
Blouse — fine matte cotton
Bow — satin silk
Skirt — premium matte woven fabric
Notebook — premium white leather
Metallic hardware — Champagne Gold

The outfit must never appear glossy, synthetic or inexpensive.

Luxury is communicated through material quality, proportion and restraint rather than ornamentation.
